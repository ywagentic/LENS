# Project year sources

Project years refer to completion, rather than recording or article publication dates.

- **Yongqing Fang Renewal — 2017.** Lab D+H’s landscape project was constructed between July 2016 and January 2017. This date does not describe every later phase of the wider district. [Project description](https://www.gooood.cn/yongqing-fang-lab-dh.htm).
- **Huacheng Square — 2010.** The Guangdong Foreign Affairs Office records completion and commissioning in 2010. This refers to the square and central-axis landscape, not all surrounding towers. [Project information](https://www.gdfao.gov.cn/zwgk/zdly/gzscfd/content/post_1333472.html).
