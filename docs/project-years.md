# Project year sources

The catalogue `year` is the completion year, not the article publication or capture year. Updated directly in the Google Sheet on 2026-09-12.

- ID 9, Yongqing Fang Renewal: **2017**. Lab D+H's supplied project description lists design March–December 2016 and construction July 2016–January 2017. This date refers to the Lab D+H landscape project, not every later phase of the wider district. Source: https://www.gooood.cn/yongqing-fang-lab-dh.htm
- ID 20, Huacheng Square: **2010**. Guangdong Foreign Affairs Office states construction began in 2006 and completion / commissioning occurred in 2010. This is the square / CBD central-axis landscape, not completion of all surrounding CBD towers. Source: https://www.gdfao.gov.cn/zwgk/zdly/gzscfd/content/post_1333472.html
