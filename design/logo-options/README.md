# LENS logo options

Three concept presentations made with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-09-12.
These are raster design candidates, not final vector logos, and are not installed in the website navigation.

1. `01-panorama.png` — panoramic viewing field, two open arcs and a sampled viewpoint.
2. `02-spatial-index.png` — four collection modules with one green circular sample.
3. `03-wordmark.png` — lowercase wordmark, green open e, compact editorial direction.

The brand is a contemporary urban landscape VR database, not an architectural practice.
Palette: deep green #1F8A5B, near-black, white. Layouts include small-scale applications.

## Prompts

### 01 — initial

Use case: logo-brand. One premium contemporary minimalist identity concept for LENS, a VR DATABASE OF CONTEMPORARY URBAN LANDSCAPE SPACES, not an architecture firm. Direction A: panoramic field of view. Design a compact symbol of two elegant horizontally stretched open arcs, suggesting the upper and lower limits of an immersive panoramic viewing field, with one small offset square representing a sampled viewpoint. Carefully balance this simple geometry; no ellipse ring wrapping a planet, no globe, no eye/pupil, no camera aperture, no buildings, no roofs, no walls, no leaves, no goggles, no 3D. Pair symbol to the left of exact wordmark 'LENS' in refined regular-weight contemporary sans-serif, beautifully kerned, open and light without hairlines. Green #1F8A5B for symbol and almost-black #171717 for wordmark. FLAT VECTOR-LIKE ART, SOLID COLORS ONLY. Pure opaque WHITE #FFFFFF entire background, absolutely no dark background, glow, lighting, texture, gradients or shadows. Landscape 3:2 presentation canvas. One large centered horizontal lockup filling about 65 percent of canvas width. Under it at small scale show the identical symbol in black, and the identical complete logo at navigation-bar scale. Same identity repeated, not alternate designs. No text other than LENS, no explanations, no watermark, no mockup objects. Sophisticated open, spatial, digital-library character, not corporate construction branding.

### 02

Create a DISTINCT second logo direction for the SAME LENS urban landscape VR database. Use the supplied board ONLY as reference for pure white background, flat green and black printing, clean typographic quality, and presentation layout. Replace its panorama symbol entirely. Direction B 'spatial index': a compact 2-by-2 arrangement of four simple image-sample tiles, with generous deliberate white gutters and one tile represented by a solid circular sample instead of a square. The four modules should make one coherent small mark, visually balanced, not a Microsoft logo imitation. Squares have restrained subtly rounded corners. Use near-black for three modules, deep green #1F8A5B for the circular module. An economical visual library / collected viewpoints idea, no buildings, no roofs, no arrows, no camera, no eye, no ornamental illustration. Place next to exact uppercase wordmark LENS in regular-weight contemporary grotesk, excellent kerning, much lighter than bold corporate identity. ### 03

Create a THIRD genuinely distinct logo direction for LENS, an accessible contemporary urban landscape VR database. Keep every letter immediately readable. Carefully balanced compact spacing and gently round curves, straight simple l, no swoosh, no serif, no italic, no camera/eye/building/leaf icon, no extra separate symbol next to wordmark. Aim for calm contemporary editorial digital archive identity, much softer and more compact than the other two options.
